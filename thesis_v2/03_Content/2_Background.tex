Calibration experiment: framing
- A sensitivity profile, not a noise band
- it shows which kinds of differences the model registers and how strongly
- by comparing contrast types against each other: an ordering (paraphrase vs negation vs same topic with a different claim vs unrelated)
- Not a threshold that corpus distances must exceed
- group-level comparisons in the corpus need their own checks (for example permutation tests)
- the experiment informs interpretation, it does not replace those checks
- Results reported as distributions per contrast type
- near-identical pairs as the top anchor, unrelated pairs as the bottom anchor
- because raw cosines in bge-m3 do not span the full 0 to 1 range
- Triplets where possible
- is the edited sentence closer to the original than a different claim on the same topic?
- Note: the pair list was written with an external LLM, Claude Opus 5.5, from my prompt, then curated by me
- prompt and details in the notice of AI use, \ref{sec:ai_notice_calibration}

[TO REVIEW: aim]
- Before interpreting distances in the corpus: test what cosine similarity in bge-m3 responds to
- hand-written sentence pairs, each differing in one controlled way
- The aim is not a significance threshold
- it is which kinds of differences (meaning, stance, negation, language, wording) the model registers, and how strongly relative to one another

Precedents
- Controlled sentence pairs and triplets are an established way to probe sentence embeddings
- Zhu, Li and de Melo 2018 (zhuExploringSemanticProperties2018)
- sentence triplets with controlled edits: negation, synonym substitution, passivisation, argument reordering
- how these changes affect embedding similarity
- Nikolaev and Padó 2023 (nikolaevRepresentationBiasesSentence2023) [VERIFY pages: 3701 to 3716 or 3683 to 3698]
- controlled sentences with modern sentence transformers
- cosine similarity driven more by overlap in the nouns than by predicates or syntactic roles
- Optional: the 0 to 5 graded similarity scale of the SemEval STS tasks, Cer et al. 2017 (cerSemEval2017Task12017)
- level 1 means not equivalent, but on the same topic

[TO REVIEW: calibration results]
Calibration results
- Run: bge-m3 via Ollama, 249 pairs, all kept after curation
- Ordering of medians, cult set
- near-identical 0.98, singular vs plural 0.98, paraphrase 0.90, register shift 0.88, label swap 0.88, translation 0.87, opposite stance 0.84, negation 0.84
- same topic, different claim 0.63, unrelated 0.52, name vs description 0.37
- Cosines never go near 0: unrelated pairs still sit around 0.5, so only the ordering is read
- Pre-registered expectations
- holds: the anchors in order; opposite stance closer than a different claim; translation near paraphrase; singular and plural near paraphrase; matched names above mismatched ones; the choir control follows the same ordering
- fails: negation was expected closer than paraphrase, it sits slightly below (0.84 vs 0.90)
- Triplets: every edit (paraphrase, stance, negation, translation, number) stays closer to its base than a different claim on the same topic, 98 of 98
- what is claimed counts more than how it is said or whether it is affirmed or denied
- The label barely matters: swapping cult for sect, choir or company keeps 0.88
- Register shift moves similarity about as much as a paraphrase
- A bare name against a description of the group scores below two unrelated sentences
- matches the earlier name-form finding: names and descriptions are not comparable in this model
- Translation slightly lower for cult sentences than for choir sentences (0.87 vs 0.93): a hint that secte is not neutral, too few pairs to claim it

Masked-entity test
- 15 entities named in at least 3 segments from at least 2 interviews; query is the bare name, pool is the 430 participant segments
- With the name: strong retrieval (precision 0.71 to 1.0 for Manson family, Raëlism, Scientology, Jehovah's Witnesses, Satanists; chance about 0.02)
- With the name masked: precision near 0 for most entities
- retrieval rested mainly on the name
- Masked segments still rank better than chance (mean rank about 50 to 170 of 430, chance about 215), except the Ku Klux Klan
- some content remains beyond the name
- Consequence: entity results in Results are read from the quotes, not from proximity to the name
