none were merged, see below):

- Aug 16 at 15-17 (1).txt

ON MERGING
Two pairs of files in this batch share close timestamps and a naming pattern that
could suggest split recordings ("Aug 16 at 15-14 (1)" / "Aug 16 at 15-17 (1)", and
"Aug 22 at 20-57" / "Aug 22 at 21-01"). In both cases the demographics captured in
each file are different (different age/gender/nationality), meaning they are two
distinct interviewees recorded back-to-back rather than two takes of the same
interview — so all seven files were kept separate.

WHAT WAS DONE TO EACH FILE
1. Speaker turns: the raw auto-transcripts have no diarization at all — interviewer
   and interviewee speech runs together in undifferentiated paragraphs. Every file
   here has been split into "Interviewer:" / "Interviewee:" turns by reading for
   who's asking vs. answering. This is a best-effort reconstruction, not a verified
   transcript; several files (especially "Aug 18 at 16-45," "Aug 21 at 19-32," and
   "Aug 23 at 12-13") have heavier cross-talk or garbling and correspondingly
   lower-confidence turn boundaries, flagged in each file's own notes.
2. Light cleanup: filler words, false starts, and obvious repeated words were
   smoothed for readability without changing meaning. Recurring, unambiguous
   ASR mishearings of "secte"/"cult" (e.g. "called," "culture," "caught," "cette
   secte / insecte" self-corrections) were corrected silently where the pattern was
   clear; genuinely uncertain words or phrases were left in place, flagged inline
   with [?].
3. Likely mistakes: less certain corrections (misheard names, garbled phrases) are
   flagged inline with [?] and explained in a "TRANSCRIPTION / EDITING NOTES"
   section at the bottom of each file. The most notable ones this batch, worth a
   second look against the original audio:
   - "fatalakulan" / "the Guillain culture" → almost certainly Fethullah Gülen and
     the Gülen movement/cult (Aug 16, 15:17 interview).
   - "tamzil" → likely Menzil, a real closed religious community based in Menzil
     village, Adıyaman province, southeastern Turkey — confirmed via web search to
     match "whole town for themselves, getting in and out controlled" (Aug 16,
     15:17 interview).
   - "le truc du cercle solaire" → likely a reference to l'Ordre du Temple Solaire
     (the Solar Temple), the real cult behind a series of 1990s mass
     suicides/murders in Switzerland, France, and Canada (Aug 22, 20:57 interview).
   - A group in the south of France described as being in a TV series/documentary
     and associated with singing, with restricted freedom to leave, is very
     plausibly the Twelve Tribes community (known in France as "Tabitha's Place,"
     based in Toulouse) — confirmed via web search as a strong contextual match,
     though the interviewee's actual words for the name were too garbled to
     recover (Aug 22, 21:01 interview).
   - "Charles Manson NCAA" → proposed as "Charles Manson, and CIA" given the
     interview immediately goes on to discuss MK Ultra (Aug 16, 15:14 interview).
   - "La secte de rail" → Raël/Raëlism, corrected silently (same recurring name
     mishearing pattern seen in a previous batch's "realism" → "Raëlism").
4. Demographics: each file's header lists the interviewee's stated age, gender,
   main language, and nationality where the raw transcript captured them clearly.
   Several interviews in this batch are missing one or more fields (flagged
   individually in that file's header/notes): gender wasn't captured for the
   Aug 21 interview; nationality wasn't captured for the Aug 22, 20:57 interview;
   and the Aug 23 interview has an unreliable, possibly joking, stated age (97)
   and an unconfirmed (though implied) gender.

WHAT WASN'T DONE
- No audio was available to verify any of this — everything above is inferred from
  the raw text alone. Anywhere the notes flag uncertainty, treat it as worth
  double-checking against the original recordings if it matters for the thesis.
- Nothing was removed for content reasons; a few short unintelligible fragments
  (e.g. a garbled opening line before the demographic questions in two files) were
  dropped rather than guessed at, and one already-masked/illegible word in the raw
  source ("p*****," Aug 22 20:57 interview) was reproduced as-is.
- Two interviews (Aug 16, 15:17 and Aug 22, 20:57) appear to end somewhat abruptly,
  without a clear closing exchange, and no continuation file exists for either —
  both are treated as complete standalone interviews per the merging rule above,
  but the abruptness is flagged in each file's own notes rather than smoothed over.
