Several raw files (Aug 8 at 14-20, Aug 8 at 21-28, Aug 12 at 12-31, Aug 13 at

gap in between where the recording was restarted — these are kept as one
continuous interview per file, as they already are in the raw folder.

WHAT WAS DONE TO EACH FILE
1. Speaker turns: the raw auto-transcripts have no diarization at all — interviewer
   and interviewee speech runs together in undifferentiated paragraphs. Every file
   here has been split into "Interviewer:" / "Interviewee:" turns by reading for
   who's asking vs. answering. This is a best-effort reconstruction, not a verified
   transcript — treat turn boundaries as likely-correct, not certain.
2. Light cleanup: filler words, false starts, and obvious repeated words were
   smoothed for readability without changing meaning. Recurring, unambiguous
   ASR errors (e.g. "cold"/"call"/"color"/"college"/"clues" for "cult") were
   corrected silently since the pattern is consistent across the whole batch.
3. Likely mistakes: less certain corrections (misheard names, garbled phrases,
   ambiguous words) are flagged inline with [?] and explained in a
   "TRANSCRIPTION / EDITING NOTES" section at the bottom of each file, rather than
   silently changed. A few notable ones worth a second look against the original
   audio:
   - "realism" → almost certainly "Raëlism" (Aug 13 interview)
   - "Gomez Gomez" / "Holy Hell" / "Buddha field cult" → the real cult
     "Buddhafield," led by Michel Rostand (born Jaime Gomez), subject of the 2016
     documentary Holy Hell (Aug 13 interview)
   - "corarev" → almost certainly Andrei Kuraev, a real, publicly defrocked
     Russian Orthodox deacon (Aug 9, 20:48 interview)
   - "Cuckoo's Klan" → Ku Klux Klan (Aug 8, 21:37 interview)
   - "Butch" → likely "Deutsch" (Aug 12 interview)
   - "Miss Brown" → likely "Missbrauch" (German for "abuse"/"misuse") (Aug 8,
     21:28 interview)
4. Demographics: each file's header lists the interviewee's stated age, gender,
   main language, and nationality where the raw transcript captured them clearly.
   A few interviews are missing one or more of these (flagged in that file); for
   Aug 12, age and gender weren't in the raw audio-to-text at all and were filled
   in from the interviewer's own knowledge instead.

WHAT WASN'T DONE
- No audio was available to verify any of this — everything above is inferred from
  the raw text alone. Anywhere the notes flag uncertainty, treat it as worth
  double-checking against the original recordings if it matters for your thesis.
- Nothing was removed for content reasons; a couple of very short garbled passages
  (a few seconds each) were left as gaps rather than guessed at outright.
