Interviewee: A group of people that are blindly devoted, or blindly follow a belief without questioning it.

Interviewer: Okay. Do you have examples of such groups?

Interviewee: Religion.

Interviewee: Um, I remember watching a documentary about a cult. I think it was in Oregon or something — they were following this guy, and he was super... I feel like usually the leaders are super abusive. Um, but nothing in specific — I would say mostly religion.

Interviewer: Okay. Do you remember the name of the one in Oregon?

Interviewee: No, I just remember watching it a long time ago. I just remember watching the documentary, and they were living in like one big property with a lot of land, and they were camping, and it was supposed to be spiritual, but he was abusing them emotionally and in other ways. And I don't remember the name.

Interviewee: Funny thing — a friend of mine who used to play polo, she played polo for like 16 years... she used to say that polo is a cult.

Interviewer: Yeah, why do you think polo could be a cult?

Interviewee: I don't think it's a cult. I think... I think the fact that we get obsessed with it — if it's a very enclosed, non-welcoming community, it could make it feel like that — but I don't think there's a leader that we follow.

Interviewer: Yeah, yeah — so she was saying it as a joke, more so?

Interviewee: Yeah, because it's like, once you start, it's hard to leave.
