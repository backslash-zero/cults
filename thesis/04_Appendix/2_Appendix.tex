\chapter{Literature and Terminology Corpus}\label{ch:literature_corpus}

This chapter documents the wider research corpus — collected literature,
dictionary/thesaurus entries, and custom working terms — kept separate from
both the interview corpus (\ref{ch:interview_corpus}) and the formal
bibliography (\texttt{05\_Literature\_and\_Index/Bibliography.bib}). Everything
here is generated from \texttt{corpus/} (alongside this LaTeX source, inside
\texttt{thesis/}) by \texttt{corpus/scripts/build\_corpus.py}; see
\texttt{corpus/README.md} for the full pipeline and update procedure.

\section{Naming Conventions}

Every item in the corpus (across all four categories, plus the interview
corpus) gets a lowercase, hyphen-only ID that is also its LaTeX cross-reference
label, so anything can be cited from anywhere in the thesis and traced back to
its source file and metadata entry unambiguously.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Type & Scheme & Example \\
\midrule
Interview & \texttt{<batch>-<slug>} & \texttt{b1-aug05-1650}, \texttt{ig-02} \\
Literature & \texttt{lit-<firstauthor><year>-<title-slug>} & \texttt{lit-durkheim1912-elementary-forms} \\
Dictionary/thesaurus entry & \texttt{dict-<source-slug>-<term-slug>} & \texttt{dict-thesoz-sekte} \\
Custom term & \texttt{term-<slug>} & \texttt{term-semantic-drift} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Two further ID families are reserved for later stages of the pipeline (text
chunks and named entities extracted from the literature and interview corpora)
but are not yet generated: \texttt{<parent-id>-chunk-<n>} and
\texttt{<parent-id>-ent-<n>}.
