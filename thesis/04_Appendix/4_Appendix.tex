\chapter{Interview Corpus}\label{ch:interview_corpus}

This chapter presents the structured corpus of cult-word-association interviews
described in the interview protocol (\ref{sec:interview_protocol}, see also the
survey methodology chapter, \ref{ch:survey_methodology}). Each interview is
transcribed, proofread, and — for French-language interviews — translated into
English; the full pipeline and the machine-readable database this chapter is
generated from are documented in
\texttt{Interviews/Corpus/scripts/build\_corpus.py} and
\texttt{Interviews/README.md}.
